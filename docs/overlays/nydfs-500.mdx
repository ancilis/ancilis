## Overview

23 NYCRR Part 500 is the NYDFS cybersecurity regulation binding on DFS-regulated financial services "Covered Entities." Its Second Amendment (effective Nov 1, 2023, phasing in through Nov 1, 2025) added governance, asset-management, 72-hour incident and 24-hour ransomware notice, annual compliance certification, and Class A enhanced controls.

The `nydfs-500` overlay packages AKSI control evidence against Part 500 and maps each AKSI control to the relevant section citations (e.g., 500.4, 500.6, 500.7(a), 500.13(a), 500.16(a)(1), 500.17(a)). Part 500 is an entity-level program regulation, so most mappings are scoped to the agent-runtime slice (agent identity, authorization, audit trail, containment, notification-clock start, recovery) rather than the full entity-level obligation.

<Warning>
This overlay is a scaffold mapping that is not yet backed by runtime evidence. Every control's `evidence_requirements` list is currently empty, and the source mapping is marked "REVIEW-FIRST, not production-asserted" with most mappings carried as `partial` (and several as `verified=false`). Treat coverage as indicative only.
</Warning>

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Operates within or on behalf of a DFS-regulated financial services "Covered Entity"
- Handles nonpublic information (`DC-NPI`), financial data (`DC-FIN`), personally identifiable information (`DC-PII`), or financial-customer information (`DC-FCI`)
- Participates in a system subject to NYDFS Part 500 governance, audit-trail, or incident-notification obligations
- Must start or feed the 72-hour incident / 24-hour ransomware notification clock at the agent-runtime layer

---

## Activation

Activate the overlay explicitly in your configuration:

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - nydfs-500
```

**Data classification trigger** — because this overlay has `trigger_type: data_classification`, it also auto-activates when your agent declares handling of the relevant data classes (`DC-NPI`, `DC-FIN`, `DC-PII`, `DC-FCI`) via `my_agent_handles`. See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for how declared data classes map to overlay activation.

## Retention & Jurisdiction

**Jurisdiction:** United States — New York State (DFS-regulated financial services "Covered Entities").

**Regime version:** Second Amendment, adopted/effective November 1, 2023 (staggered transitional periods through November 1, 2025).

**Retention:** The source overlay does not define an `evidence_retention_minimum_days` value. Part 500 itself imposes retention obligations in the mapped sections — for example, 500.6 requires audit trails to be securely maintained for at least five years, and 500.17(b) requires five-year retention of documentation supporting the annual compliance certification. Configure evidence retention to meet the longest applicable Part 500 obligation for your Covered Entity.
