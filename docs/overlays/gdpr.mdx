## Overview

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) governs the processing of personal data of individuals in the European Union, setting requirements for lawful basis, data subject rights, security of processing, breach notification, and data protection by design and by default.

The `gdpr` overlay packages AKSI control evidence against GDPR requirements. It is activated by personal data, health data, children's data, or biometric data declarations, and maps each AKSI control to the relevant GDPR Articles covering lawful basis, data subject rights, security of processing, breach notification, and data protection by design.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Processes personal data (`DC-PII`) of EU data subjects
- Handles health or patient data (`DC-PHI`)
- Processes children's data (`DC-MINOR`)
- Processes biometric data (`DC-BIO`)

---

## Activation

Enable the overlay explicitly in your configuration:

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - gdpr
```

**Data classification trigger** — because this is a `data_classification` overlay, it also auto-activates when your agent declares that it handles the relevant data classifications (`DC-PII`, `DC-PHI`, `DC-MINOR`, or `DC-BIO`) via `my_agent_handles`. See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for how declared classifications drive overlay activation.

## Retention & Jurisdiction

- **Jurisdiction:** `eu` — the overlay applies to processing of personal data of EU data subjects.
- **Evidence retention minimum:** 365 days. Retain control evidence for at least this period to support GDPR accountability and supervisory-authority requests.

Controls without overlay-specific adjustments inherit the base AKSI common-control definitions; this overlay adds GDPR-specific evidence fields and threshold adjustments on top of that catalog.
