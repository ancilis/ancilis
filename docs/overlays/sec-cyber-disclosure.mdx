## Overview

The SEC Cybersecurity Risk Management, Strategy, Governance, and Incident Disclosure Rules (Regulation S-K Item 106; Form 8-K Item 1.05; Form 10-K Item 1C / Form 20-F / Form 6-K for foreign private issuers) are a public-company **disclosure** regime, not a prescriptive security-controls standard: they require timely Form 8-K Item 1.05 reporting of material cyber incidents (within 4 business days of the materiality determination) and annual Reg S-K Item 106 / Form 10-K Item 1C descriptions of cyber risk-management processes and board/management governance. The agent-responsibility boundary is narrow and indirect: an agent runtime can detect/escalate incidents and start and preserve evidence.

The `sec-cyber-disclosure` overlay packages AKSI control evidence against the parts of this regime that an agent runtime can actually contribute to — incident detection and escalation, the regulated notification clock, tamper-evident audit trails, governance accountability, and third-party/dependency risk identification — and maps each control to its Reg S-K Item 106 and Form 8-K Item 1.05 citations.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Operates within an SEC-registered public company / Exchange Act registrant (including foreign private issuers)
- Handles material non-public information, financial data, or personal information that could factor into a material cybersecurity incident
- Can detect, escalate, or preserve evidence for cybersecurity incidents subject to Form 8-K Item 1.05 disclosure
- Contributes to the cyber risk-management and governance processes described under Reg S-K Item 106

## Activation

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - sec-cyber-disclosure
```

**Data classification trigger** — because this overlay's `trigger_type` is `data_classification`, it also auto-activates when your agent declares (via `my_agent_handles`) that it handles data classified under `DC-MNPI`, `DC-FIN`, or `DC-PII`:

```yaml
my_agent_handles:
  - DC-MNPI
```

See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for the full classification code reference.

## Jurisdiction & Retention

**Jurisdiction:** United States (SEC-registered public companies / Exchange Act registrants, including foreign private issuers).

The overlay definition does not specify an `evidence_retention_minimum_days` value; apply your organization's record-retention policy for cybersecurity-incident and disclosure evidence.

This overlay is a review-first mapping: most control mappings are marked `partial` and several are not yet verified against runtime evidence. Treat coverage as indicative and confirm each mapping before relying on it for disclosure.
