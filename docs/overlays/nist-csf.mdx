## Overview

The `nist-csf` overlay maps AKSI control evidence to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 functions and categories. CSF is the most widely adopted cybersecurity framework in the US private sector and provides the foundation for many other compliance programs including FedRAMP, CMMC, and sector-specific regulations.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Operates within an organization using NIST CSF as its security framework
- Needs to demonstrate cybersecurity risk management to enterprise customers
- Is part of a broader NIST CSF implementation program
- Requires alignment with a framework that maps to multiple other standards

---

## Activation

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - nist-csf
```

## NIST CSF 2.0 Functions

CSF 2.0 organizes controls into six functions:

| Function | Abbreviation | Purpose |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| Govern | GV | Establish risk strategy and accountability |
| Identify | ID | Understand assets and risks |
| Protect | PR | Implement safeguards |
| Detect | DE | Identify cybersecurity events |
| Respond | RS | Take action after detection |
| Recover | RC | Restore capabilities after incidents |

| **PR-02** (Scope Confinement) | Protect | Identity Management & Access Control (PR.AA) | PR.AA-03, PR.AA-05 |

| **DE-01** (Dependencies) | Identify | Risk Assessment (ID.RA) | ID.RA-01, ID.RA-07 |

## Evidence Requirements

### PR-01 — Identity & Authorization (PR.AA)
- PR.AA-01: Identities and credentials for authorized users, services, and hardware are managed
- PR.AA-02: Identities are proofed and bound to credentials
- Agent access permissions reviewed periodically

### PR-02 — Scope Confinement (PR.AA)
- PR.AA-03: Users, services, and hardware are authenticated
- PR.AA-05: Access permissions and authorizations are managed
- Agent operates with least-privilege permissions (least functionality)

### PR-03 — Tool Provenance (PR.PS, DE.CM)
- PR.PS-01: Configuration management practices are established
- DE.CM-01: Networks and network services are monitored
- DE.CM-03: Personnel activity and technology usage are monitored

### PR-04 — Data Exposure (PR.DS)
- PR.DS-01: Data-at-rest is protected
- PR.DS-02: Data-in-transit is protected
- PR.DS-10: Data are managed throughout their life cycle

### PR-05 — Audit Trail (DE.AE)
- DE.AE-02: Potentially adverse events are analyzed to better characterize them
- DE.AE-03: Information is correlated from multiple sources
- DE.AE-06: Information on adverse events is provided to authorized staff and tools

### DE-01 — Dependency Vulnerabilities (ID.RA)
- ID.RA-01: Vulnerabilities in assets are identified, validated, and recorded
- ID.RA-07: Changes and exceptions are managed, assessed for risk impact, recorded, and tracked

## Example Configuration

```yaml
agent:
  name: enterprise-ops-agent
  description: "Enterprise operational automation agent"

security:
  mode: audit

compliance:
  overlays:
    - nist-csf
  evidence:
    retention_days: 365

scan:
  dependencies:
    enabled: true
    severity_threshold: high
```
