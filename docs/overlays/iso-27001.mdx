## Overview

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is the international standard for information security management systems (ISMS). Its Annex A defines security controls across four themes — Organizational (A.5), People (A.6), Physical (A.7), and Technological (A.8) — that certified organizations must apply where relevant to their risk profile.

The `iso-27001` overlay packages AKSI control evidence against the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls most relevant to AI agent deployments. It maps each AKSI control to the Annex A controls it supports, treating AI agents as information assets within the ISMS scope, and their tools and MCP servers as cloud services or suppliers under A.5.23 and A.5.19. This overlay covers Annex A technical controls only — it does not cover the full ISMS clauses 4–10, which are organizational requirements.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Operates within an organization holding or pursuing ISO/IEC 27001 certification
- Must produce evidence for applicable Annex A controls ahead of a certification or surveillance audit
- Is treated as an information asset within the organization's ISMS scope
- Handles data requiring documented confidentiality, integrity, and availability controls

---

## Activation

Activate the overlay explicitly in your project configuration:

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - iso-27001
```

**Data classification trigger** — because this overlay has a `data_classification` trigger type, it also auto-activates when your agent declares (via `my_agent_handles`) that it handles any of the data classifications it is triggered by: `DC-GEN`, `DC-AI`, `DC-PII`, `DC-PHI`, `DC-FIN`, `DC-GOV`, or `DC-CUI`. See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for how to declare the data your agent handles.

## Retention & Jurisdiction

- **Evidence retention minimum:** 1095 days (3 years), aligning with ISO/IEC 27001's annual surveillance audits and 3-year recertification cycle.
- **Jurisdiction:** International (ISO certification; adopted by 170+ countries).
- **Human oversight:** required — human review of AI agent risk assessments and compliance monitoring outputs, with documented management review of ISMS effectiveness including AI agent security controls (basis: A.5.35, A.5.36, A.8.16).
