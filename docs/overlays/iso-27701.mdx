## Overview

ISO/IEC 27701:2019 is the ISO privacy information management system (PIMS) extension to ISO/IEC 27001 and 27002, adding PII-controller controls (clause 7 / Annex A) and PII-processor controls (clause 8 / Annex B). It is a regulation-agnostic PIMS; its Annex D maps to EU GDPR.

The `iso-27701` overlay packages AKSI control evidence against the ISO/IEC 27701:2019 Annex A / Annex B structure, mapping each AKSI control to the relevant clause references for agents that process PII as controller or processor.

<Warning>
This overlay is a review-first scaffold mapping, not yet backed by runtime evidence. The 2019 edition targeted here was withdrawn on 2025-10-14 and superseded by the standalone ISO/IEC 27701:2025 (transition deadline ~Oct 2028); mappings reflect the 2019 structure and should be re-verified against 2025 if the target migrates. Coverage is indicative: most control mappings are marked partial and many are unverified, and most PIMS obligations (lawful basis, consent capture, privacy notices, PIAs, DPAs) remain org/legal responsibilities outside the agent boundary. Treat this overlay as a crosswalk, not a production-asserted compliance claim.
</Warning>

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Processes personal information (PII) as a controller or processor
- Operates within an organization running an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS extending to a PIMS
- Handles records of processing, transfer governance, or data-subject request workflows
- Needs an agent-runtime crosswalk to ISO/IEC 27701:2019 Annex A / Annex B controls

---

## Activation

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - iso-27701
```

**Data classification trigger** — this overlay also auto-activates when your agent declares, via `my_agent_handles`, that it handles data classified under `DC-PII`, `DC-NPI`, or `DC-MINOR`. See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for the classification codes and declaration syntax.

## Retention & Jurisdiction

**Jurisdiction:** International (ISO/IEC). ISO/IEC 27701 is designed as a regulation-agnostic PIMS; its Annex D maps to EU GDPR.

**Evidence retention:** This overlay does not specify a minimum evidence-retention period. Set retention to satisfy the regulatory regime(s) your PIMS operates under (for example, an applicable GDPR or sector requirement) rather than relying on a value from this crosswalk.
