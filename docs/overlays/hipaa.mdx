## Overview

The HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR Part 164) sets the requirements for safeguarding the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic protected health information (ePHI) held or transmitted by covered entities and their business associates.

The `hipaa` overlay packages AKSI control evidence against those requirements. It maps each AKSI control to the relevant HIPAA Security Rule administrative, physical, and technical safeguards, and adds healthcare-specific evidence fields and threshold adjustments on top of the AKSI common-control catalog. Controls without overlay-specific adjustments inherit the base AKSI definitions.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Creates, receives, maintains, or transmits electronic protected health information (ePHI)
- Handles health records or patient data
- Operates as, or on behalf of, a business associate under a Business Associate Agreement (BAA)
- Is part of a system subject to the HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR Part 164)

---

## Activation

Activate the overlay explicitly in your agent configuration:

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - hipaa
```

**Data classification trigger** — because this overlay's `trigger_type` is `data_classification`, it also auto-activates when your agent declares that it handles the relevant classified data (`DC-PHI`) via `my_agent_handles`:

```yaml
my_agent_handles:
  - DC-PHI
```

See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for how classification declarations drive overlay activation.

## Retention & Jurisdiction

- **Jurisdiction:** US
- **Evidence retention minimum:** 2190 days (6 years), aligning with the HIPAA Security Rule documentation retention requirement (164.316(b)(2)).
