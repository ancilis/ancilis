## Overview

The EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847) sets horizontal essential cybersecurity requirements (Annex I: Part I product properties points 1 and 2(a)–(m); Part II vulnerability-handling points (1)–(8)) and reporting duties (Art. 14) for manufacturers of products with digital elements. It entered into force 10 Dec 2024, with reporting obligations applying from 11 Sep 2026 and full application from 11 Dec 2027. CRA obligations attach to the **manufacturer** and the **product** as placed on the market, so AKSI controls map only to the agent-runtime slice (secure-by-design properties, vulnerability handling).

The `eu_cra` overlay packages AKSI control evidence against the CRA essential requirements and reporting duties, mapping each in-scope AKSI control to its relevant Annex I points and Articles. The overlay is a `certification`-type overlay scoped to the **European Union** jurisdiction.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Is part of a product with digital elements placed on the EU market
- Is operated by a manufacturer subject to CRA essential cybersecurity requirements (Annex I)
- Must support CRA vulnerability-handling and coordinated-disclosure obligations
- Is in scope of CRA reporting duties (Art. 14) for actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents

<Warning>
  This overlay is built from a REVIEW-FIRST crosswalk (PROD-REPAIRS D3, aksi-ultra `eu-cra.crosswalk.json`). Most control mappings are marked **partial** and several are **not yet verified**; no control carries evidence requirements in the overlay definition. Treat coverage as **indicative**, not production-asserted, and confirm scope against the regulation before relying on it for conformity.
</Warning>

---

## Activation

Activate the overlay explicitly in your project configuration:

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - eu_cra
```

This is a `certification`-type overlay: it does not auto-activate from declared data handling and must be enabled explicitly. (For reference, the crosswalk associates the regime with the `DC-CRIT`, `DC-IP`, and `DC-PII` data classifications — see [Data Classification](/data-classification).)

## Retention & Jurisdiction

- **Jurisdiction:** European Union — Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (OJ 20 Nov 2024). In force 10 Dec 2024; reporting obligations (Art. 14) apply from 11 Sep 2026; full application from 11 Dec 2027.
- **Evidence retention:** The `eu_cra` overlay definition does not set a minimum evidence-retention period. Set `compliance.evidence.retention_days` to satisfy your applicable CRA conformity, support-period, and reporting obligations.
