## Overview

NIST AI 600-1 (final, July 2024; DOI 10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1) is a cross-sectoral companion Profile to the AI RMF 1.0. It enumerates 12 generative-AI risk categories and 200+ voluntary suggested actions keyed to AI RMF Govern/Map/Measure/Manage subcategories (e.g., GV-1.6, MS-2.7, MG-2.4). It is guidance, not regulation, so the mappings here reflect alignment-to-suggested-actions rather than mandatory conformity. The agent-responsibility-boundary caveat applies throughout: AKSI controls own the agent-runtime slice (identity, action authorization, isolation, output/security evaluation, kill-switch, audit) while organization-wide obligations remain outside the runtime.

The `nist_ai_600_1_genai_profile` overlay packages AKSI control evidence against the AI RMF subcategories referenced by the GenAI Profile, mapping each AKSI control to the Govern/Map/Measure/Manage subcategories it aligns with.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Is a generative-AI system in scope of the NIST AI 600-1 GenAI Profile
- Operates under an AI RMF 1.0 risk-management program (Govern/Map/Measure/Manage)
- Supports US federal AI policy or procurement that cross-references NIST AI guidance
- Needs agent-runtime evidence aligned to the GenAI Profile's suggested actions

<Warning>
This overlay is a **review-first scaffold mapping** that is not yet backed by runtime evidence — every control in the source profile carries an empty `evidence_requirements` set, and the source notes flag the mapping as REVIEW-FIRST, not production-asserted (many entries are `verified: false`). Treat the coverage shown below as indicative alignment to the AI RMF subcategories, not as asserted runtime conformity.
</Warning>

## Activation

Activate explicitly in your `ancilis.yaml`:

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - nist_ai_600_1_genai_profile
```

**Data classification trigger** — because this overlay's `trigger_type` is `data_classification`, it also auto-activates when your agent declares handling of the relevant data classes (`DC-AI`, `DC-GEN`) via `my_agent_handles`. See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for the declaration syntax and the full DC code list.

## Retention & Jurisdiction

- **Jurisdiction:** us-federal (NIST, voluntary; cross-referenced by US federal AI policy and procurement, and adopted internationally as guidance).
- **Evidence retention:** the source profile defines no overlay-specific minimum retention period — retention follows your agent's configured policy and any governing organizational or regulatory obligations.
