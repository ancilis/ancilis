## Overview

The NIS2 Directive (Directive (EU) 2022/2555) is the EU's cybersecurity framework for essential and important entities, with an EU member state transposition deadline of October 18, 2024. It applies to essential entities (energy, transport, banking, financial market infrastructure, health, drinking water, digital infrastructure) and important entities (postal, waste, chemicals, food, manufacturing, digital providers) with 50+ employees or €10M+ annual turnover. AI agent systems operating in or for NIS2-covered sectors must evidence the security measures required under Art. 21 and incident notification under Art. 23.

The `nis2` overlay packages AKSI control evidence for these obligations. It maps each AKSI control to the relevant NIS2 articles — the ten minimum security measures under Art. 21(2), management accountability under Art. 20, and incident notification timelines under Art. 23 — so that an agent's runtime evidence can be aligned to the Directive's requirements.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Operates in or for an EU essential or important entity subject to Directive (EU) 2022/2555
- Handles public administration or government data (DC-GOV)
- Handles critical infrastructure operational data (DC-CRIT)
- Must evidence Art. 21 security measures or Art. 23 incident notification

---

## Activation

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - nis2
```

**Data classification trigger** — because this overlay's `trigger_type` is `data_classification`, it also auto-activates when your agent declares handling of the relevant data via `my_agent_handles`. The overlay's `triggered_by` codes are `DC-GOV` (public administration and government data) and `DC-CRIT` (critical infrastructure operational data). See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for how to declare handled data classes.

Operators in other NIS2-covered sectors should evaluate triggering based on the data classification of their sector-specific operational data.

## Retention & Jurisdiction

- **Jurisdiction:** EU (`eu`) — Directive (EU) 2022/2555, as transposed by member states.
- **Evidence retention minimum:** 1825 days (5 years). Significant incident records, including root cause analysis under Art. 23(1)(d), should be retained for at least this period or as required by the applicable national transposition.
