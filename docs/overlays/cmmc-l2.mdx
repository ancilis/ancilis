## Overview

CMMC Level 2 aligns defense and federal contractors to the safeguarding of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), drawing its safeguarding requirements from NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3.

The `cmmc-l2` overlay is a government overlay for agents handling Controlled Unclassified Information or federal-system data that must be safeguarded to CMMC Level 2 expectations. It provides full 39 common-control AKSI coverage with CMMC Level 2 and NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3 citations or documented gap notes across governance, identification, protection, detection, response, and recovery — suitable for contractor audit preparation and C3PAO assessment readiness.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Handles Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) or federal-system data
- Operates within a system pursuing CMMC Level 2 readiness or C3PAO assessment
- Must demonstrate NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3 control evidence to a contracting officer or government customer
- Needs an assessor-ready CUI safeguarding evidence package

---

## Activation

Activate the overlay explicitly in your project configuration:

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - cmmc-l2
```

**Data classification trigger** — because this overlay's trigger type is `data_classification`, it also auto-activates when your agent declares (via `my_agent_handles`) that it handles the relevant data classes, namely `DC-CUI` (Controlled Unclassified Information) or `DC-GOV` (government / federal-system data). See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for how to declare the data your agent handles.

## Retention & Jurisdiction

- **Jurisdiction:** `us`
- **Evidence retention minimum:** 1095 days (3 years). Retain CUI safeguarding evidence for at least this period to support contractor audit preparation and C3PAO assessment readiness.
