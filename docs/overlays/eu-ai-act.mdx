## Overview

The EU AI Act is the first regulation directly governing AI agent behavior. It applies comprehensive requirements to high-risk AI systems — risk management, data governance, transparency, human oversight, and accuracy — as well as obligations for general-purpose AI (GPAI) models. Enforcement phases in over time: prohibited AI practices (Feb 2025), GPAI model obligations (Aug 2025), and high-risk AI system obligations (Aug 2026).

The `eu-ai-act` overlay packages AKSI control evidence against the relevant Articles and Annexes of the EU AI Act. It enforces strict monitoring, logging, and human oversight, and maps each AKSI control to its framework reference and the evidence the control is expected to produce. Risk classification is an operator determination — Ancilis provides the evidence package but cannot classify the risk level for the operator.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Operates in or serves the EU market
- Produces general-purpose AI outputs or model-generated content for EU-serving use
- Is part of a high-risk AI system subject to conformity assessment (Art.43) and EU database registration (Art.49)
- Handles AI-classified, biometric, or general-purpose AI output data (`DC-AI`, `DC-BIO`, `DC-GEN`)
- Requires automatic logging, post-market monitoring, and human oversight per Articles 12, 14, and 72

## Activation

Activate the overlay explicitly in your configuration:

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - eu-ai-act
```

**Data classification trigger** — because this overlay's `trigger_type` is `data_classification`, it also auto-activates when your agent declares (via `my_agent_handles`) that it handles data in the classes that trigger it: `DC-AI`, `DC-BIO`, or `DC-GEN`. Any EU-serving agent producing general-purpose AI outputs (`DC-GEN`) is subject to the GPAI provisions of the EU AI Act regardless of specific data classification. See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for how to declare handled data classes.

## Retention & Jurisdiction

- **Jurisdiction:** `eu` — applies to AI systems operating in or serving the EU market.
- **Evidence retention minimum:** 3650 days (10 years). Evidence packaged by this overlay must be retained for at least this period.
- **Human oversight:** required (Art.14) for high-risk AI systems — human overseers must be able to understand, interpret, and override system output, and intervene in or halt operation.
- **Risk classification** is an operator determination. Ancilis provides the evidence package but cannot classify the risk level on the operator's behalf.
