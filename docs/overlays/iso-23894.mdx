## Overview

ISO/IEC 23894:2023 (*Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Guidance on risk management*) is voluntary international guidance — not a certifiable requirements standard — that adapts the ISO 31000:2018 risk-management framework and process to AI, with Clause 4 principles, Clause 5 framework, Clause 6 process, and Annexes A (AI-related objectives), B (AI-related risk sources) and C (life-cycle mapping). It underpins the risk-treatment expectations of the certifiable ISO/IEC 42001.

The `iso-23894` overlay packages AKSI control evidence against the agent-runtime slice of the ISO/IEC 23894 risk process. Because the standard is guidance expressed as high-level objectives rather than discrete shall-statements, most mappings are interpretive and partial: the overlay owns the runtime instantiation of the risk process (identification, treatment, monitoring, recording) while framework-level and org-wide design activities remain out of scope.

<Warning>
This overlay is a review-first mapping that is not yet backed by runtime evidence. In the source overlay every control carries `coverage: partial` (several with `verified: false`) and no per-control evidence requirements are populated. Treat coverage as **indicative**, not production-asserted.
</Warning>

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Operates within an AI risk-management program aligned to ISO/IEC 23894:2023
- Supports or feeds an ISO/IEC 42001 management system that relies on ISO/IEC 23894 risk treatment
- Needs runtime evidence mapped to the ISO 31000-derived AI risk process (Clauses 5–6, Annexes A–C)
- Declares handling of AI-system data (`DC-AI`) under an organization's AI risk framework

## Activation

Activate the overlay explicitly in your project configuration:

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - iso-23894
```

This overlay has `trigger_type: certification` — it does not auto-activate from data-classification declarations and must be enabled explicitly. Its associated AI data class is `DC-AI` (see [Data Classification](/data-classification)).

## Jurisdiction & Retention

**Jurisdiction:** International (ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42); voluntary guidance, not certifiable; basis for ISO/IEC 42001 risk treatment.

The overlay does not declare a minimum evidence-retention period. Retention for ISO/IEC 23894-aligned evidence should follow your organization's AI risk-management and records policy (Clause 6.7, Recording and reporting) and any retention required by the certifiable ISO/IEC 42001 system it supports.
