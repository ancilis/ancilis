## Overview

SOC 2 Type II is the AICPA's Trust Services Criteria framework for service organizations, assessing whether controls are both designed appropriately and operating effectively over a 6–12 month audit period. It is the universal B2B compliance baseline — virtually every agent startup selling to enterprises needs it. The criteria span all five Trust Services Categories: Security (CC), Availability (A), Processing Integrity (PI), Confidentiality (C), and Privacy (P).

The `soc2` overlay maps each AKSI control to the relevant Trust Services Criteria, packaging the evidence requirements an auditor expects to see for each control. It is activated by general business data or personal information declarations.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Operates within a service organization selling to enterprise customers
- Handles general business data (`DC-GEN`) or personal information (`DC-PII`)
- Is part of a system pursuing or maintaining a SOC 2 Type I or Type II report
- Must demonstrate controls are operating effectively over an audit period

---

## Activation

Explicit activation in your project configuration:

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - soc2
```

**Data classification trigger** — because this overlay's `trigger_type` is `data_classification`, it also auto-activates when your agent declares handling of the relevant data classes (`DC-GEN` or `DC-PII`) via `my_agent_handles`. See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for the declaration syntax and the full DC code reference.

## Retention & Jurisdiction

- **Jurisdiction:** global
- **Evidence retention minimum:** 365 days — evidence must be retained for at least one year to support the Type II audit period and the licensed CPA firm's review.
