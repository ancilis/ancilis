## Overview

The Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA, Regulation (EU) 2022/2554) is a directly applicable EU regulation governing the ICT risk management, incident reporting, resilience testing, and third-party risk obligations of EU financial entities. Enforcement began January 17, 2025. It applies to banks, investment firms, insurance undertakings, payment institutions, electronic money institutions, crypto-asset service providers, and critical ICT third-party service providers.

The `dora` overlay packages AKSI control evidence against DORA-specific requirements for EU financial entities. It maps each AKSI control to the relevant DORA articles and provides DORA-specific evidence requirements beyond a combined GLBA/SOX/DORA bundle — including ICT incident notification timeline requirements (Art. 19), the DORA testing program (Arts. 24-26), and ICT third-party risk management (Arts. 28-30). Use it when the regulated entity is an EU financial entity and DORA-specific auditor evidence is required.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Operates within an EU financial entity subject to DORA
- Handles financial data or financial records as ICT assets supporting critical or important functions
- Is part of an ICT environment requiring DORA-specific incident notification and resilience evidence
- Supports management body accountability for ICT risk management under DORA Art. 5

## Activation

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - dora
```

**Data classification trigger** — because this overlay's trigger type is `data_classification`, it also auto-activates when your agent declares handling of the relevant data classification (`DC-FIN`) via `my_agent_handles`. See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for the declaration syntax.

## Retention & Jurisdiction

- **Jurisdiction:** EU (`eu`) — DORA is a directly applicable EU regulation requiring no national transposition.
- **Evidence retention minimum:** 1825 days (5 years), aligned with the record-retention expectation for critical function records under DORA Art. 12(6).
