## Overview

The Singapore IMDA/PDPC Model AI Governance Framework (Second Edition, 2020) is a voluntary, principle-based framework for private sector organizations deploying AI in Singapore. It is organized into four governance areas: (1) Internal Governance Structures, (2) Human Involvement in Decision-Making, (3) Operations Management, and (4) Stakeholder Communication. Its PDPC linkage makes personal data protection obligations directly regulatory, and it pairs as a strong companion signal with MAS TRM (mandatory for Singapore financial institutions).

The `imda-mgf` overlay packages AKSI control evidence and maps each AKSI control to the relevant MGF governance area, section reference, and AI Verify / Agentic MGF dimension citations.

**Use this overlay when your agent:**
- Deploys AI in Singapore under the IMDA/PDPC Model AI Governance Framework
- Handles personal data subject to Singapore PDPA / PDPC obligations in an AI context
- Operates alongside MAS TRM requirements as a Singapore financial institution
- Makes consequential AI-augmented decisions affecting individuals' rights, safety, or financial interests
- Needs documented human oversight classification for AI-augmented decision-making

## Activation

Activate the overlay explicitly in your config:

```yaml
# ancilis.yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - imda-mgf
```

**Data classification trigger** — because this overlay uses a `data_classification` trigger, it also auto-activates when your agent declares handling of the relevant data class (`DC-AI`) via `my_agent_handles`. See [Data Classification](/data-classification) for how to declare the `DC-AI` class.

## Retention & Jurisdiction

- **Jurisdiction:** SGP (Singapore)
- **Evidence retention minimum:** 1095 days

Configure retention to meet the overlay minimum:

```yaml
compliance:
  overlays:
    - imda-mgf
  evidence:
    retention_days: 1095
```
